## Safe City

**Type:** Independent software reference

**SSTM Research fork:**  
https://github.com/sstm-research/safe-city-app

**Original repository:**  
https://github.com/deegalabs/safe-city-app

Safe City is an independent location-based public infrastructure
implementation.

Its existing technical work includes research-relevant primitives around:

- maps
- geospatial interfaces
- location-based events
- real-time information
- privacy-aware public infrastructure

Safe City was not originally created as an artwork presence system.

Portable Presence reinterprets selected technical patterns for a new
research question involving physical artworks and cultural assets.

### Software attribution

The authoritative record of Safe City software authorship remains:

1. the original `deegalabs/safe-city-app` repository;
2. its Git commit history;
3. its original contributors;
4. its original license and project documentation.

The SSTM Research fork does not transfer authorship or ownership of the
Safe City implementation.

## Portable Presence

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/sstm-research/portable_presence

Portable Presence is a later research synthesis.

It investigates how artwork identity can be extended with evidence of:

- exhibition
- institutional presence
- check-in
- check-out
- storage
- loans
- returns
- location history

Example research events include:

- `EXHIBITED_AT`
- `CHECKED_IN_AT`
- `CHECKED_OUT_AT`
- `STORED_AT`
- `LOANED_TO`
- `RETURNED_TO`

Portable Presence represents this new research interpretation and does
not claim authorship of the Safe City code.

## Attribution principle

Research authorship, software authorship and institutional contribution
are distinct forms of participation.

A project used as a research reference does not automatically become
part of the authorship of a later research work.

A later research interpretation also does not transfer ownership of the
software that informed it.

Original repositories, contributors, commits, dates and licenses should
remain visible whenever possible.
